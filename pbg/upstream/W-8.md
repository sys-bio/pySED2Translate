# viva-tellurium and viva-copasi: steady-state steps have no solver settings; no Jacobian step

Repositories: viva-tellurium, viva-copasi (0.1.2).  Evidence: **read** (COPASI's step was read, not run).

* `TelluriumSteadyStateStep` calls `steadyState()` with roadrunner's defaults; the inherited tolerance keys are not used
  (see W-1).  `CopasiSteadyStateStep` calls `run_steadystate(update_model=True, ...)` with defaults.
* Neither package offers a Jacobian (full or reduced) step; roadrunner has `getFullJacobian()` / `getReducedJacobian()` and
  basico has `get_jacobian()`.

Expected: steady-state solver and tolerance options, and Jacobian steps.

Environment: Python 3.13, process-bigraph 1.8.5, bigraph-schema 1.7.0, numpy 2.2.6; Linux (x86_64). Observed 2026-10-09.

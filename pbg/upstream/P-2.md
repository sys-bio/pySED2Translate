# process-bigraph: a cycle of steps is broken silently

Repository: vivarium-collective/process-bigraph (1.8.5).  Evidence: confirmed by running (an earlier session of this work).

Supplement 1 (3.8.4) of the process-bigraph paper says "Cycles are disallowed, ensuring deterministic zero-time DAG
evaluation".  In 1.8.5 a pair of steps wired in a cycle (A reads what B writes and B reads what A writes) loads and runs;
the cycle is broken by priority and the results are arbitrary, with no error or warning.

Expected: an error at construction naming the cycle, as the specification says.

Environment: Python 3.13, process-bigraph 1.8.5, bigraph-schema 1.7.0, numpy 2.5.3; Linux (x86_64). Observed 2026-10-09.
